About Me

Hello there! I am an a senior undergraduate student at the University of Washington, where I study Computer Science and Math. I currently work with the UW Interactive Data Lab in the realm of Human-AI Interaction, focusing on how generative AI is used in the research process. In have had the honor of working with Dr. Leilani Battle at the University of Washington, Dr. Koji Yatani during my summer at the University of Tokyo, and Dr. Ta-Chen Lin during my time at Tunghai University, among others.

Beyond research, I am a lead TA for CSE 121 (Intro to Java Programming) and the Co-Chair of Q++, one of the Allen School affinity groups. In terms of personal interests, I love collecting CDs, crossword puzzles, and traveling (when I can), among other things. This site is mostly an interactive CV, so feel free to poke around. If you have any questions, want to chat, or have a research opportunity, please don't hesitate to reach out (contact info at the bottom of the page).
